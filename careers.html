CAREERS AT NOVA

Join a multidisciplinary organisation working at the forefront of scientific research, engineering, and technological development. At NOVA, our teams work together to investigate complex problems, develop new technologies, and expand our understanding of the world around us.

WHY NOVA?

NOVA provides an environment where curiosity, collaboration, and responsible innovation are central to our work. Our employees contribute to projects spanning multiple scientific disciplines, working alongside researchers, engineers, and technical specialists to turn ideas into practical results.

AREAS OF EMPLOYMENT

RESEARCH

Work across scientific disciplines to investigate new ideas, conduct experiments, analyse findings, and contribute to NOVA's research programs.

ENGINEERING

Design, develop, test, and maintain the systems and equipment required to support NOVA's research and technological projects.

TECHNICAL OPERATIONS

Support the safe and reliable operation of research facilities, laboratory systems, equipment, and technical infrastructure.

DATA & COMPUTING

Develop software, manage research data, and create computational systems that support scientific analysis and experimentation.
